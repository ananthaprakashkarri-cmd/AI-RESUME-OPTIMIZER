About AI Resume Optimizer 🤖

AI Resume Optimizer is an intelligent resume enhancement platform designed to help students and job seekers create better resumes based on specific job requirements.

Our system analyzes your resume, compares it with job descriptions, finds missing skills, calculates ATS compatibility, and generates an improved resume with the power of Artificial Intelligence.

🎯 Our Goal

To make job applications smarter by helping candidates customize their resumes for every opportunity.

⚡ Features

• ATS Score Analysis
• Skill Matching
• AI Resume Rewrite
• Cover Letter Generation

🛠 Technology

Built using AI automation workflows, n8n, LLM models, and modern web technologies.
